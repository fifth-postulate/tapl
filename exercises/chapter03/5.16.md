# 3.3.16

Let $L$ be the language of untyped arithmetic expressions and $M$ be $L$
augmented with `wrong`.

$L$ agrees with $M$ in the following way:

> Whenever an expression $l$ in $L$ is in normal form, but is not a value,
> there is an derivation of $l'$ in $M$ that directly leads to `wrong`.

## Proof

By definition of $M$.
