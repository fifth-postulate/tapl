# 3.5.5

> Spell out the induction principle used in the preceding proof, in the style
> of 3.3.4.

## Answer

The used principle of induction is *structural induction*.

> If for each term $s$, given $P(r)$ for all immediate subterms $r$ of $s$,
> we can show $P(s)$, then $P(s)$ holds for all $s$.
