# 2.2.6

> Suppose we are given a relation $R$ on a set $S$. Define the relation $R'$ as
> follows:

$$
R' = R \cup \{(s,s)|s\in S\}
$$

That is, $R'$ contains all the pairs in $R$ plus all pairs of the form $(s,s)$.
Show that $R'$ is the reflexive closure of R.

## Proof

A relation $E$ on $S$ is reflexive when $(s,s)\in E$ for all $s\in S$. In other
words, $E$ is reflexive if and only if

$$
\{(s,s)|s\in S\} \subset E
$$

By definition of $R'$ we have $\{(s,s)|s\in S\} \subset R'$ so $R'$ is reflexive.

If there is an other reflexive relation $B$ such that $R \subset B$ then also
$R' \subset B$. Since if $r\in R'$ then either $r\in R$ or $r=(s,s)$ for an
$s\in S$.
If $r\in R$ then also $r\in B$ since $R\subset B$. If $r=(s,s$) for an $s\in S$
then also $r\in B$ since $B$ is reflexive. Either way $r\in B$ hence
$$R'\subset B$$

So $R'$ is the reflexive clojure of $R$.
